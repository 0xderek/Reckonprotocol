01 — SYSTEM
Four stages, one autonomous loop.

From a raw invoice to a live, monitored position — Reckon runs the full credit lifecycle without a human underwriter in the loop.

STAGE 1

Underwrite

Upload an invoice or paste receivable data. The agent extracts terms, checks counterparty signals, and outputs a risk score with suggested terms.

→
STAGE 2

Tokenize

Approved assets are minted as onchain RWA tokens on X Layer — one click, ERC-4626-style vault shares backed by the underlying receivable.

→
STAGE 3

Manage

Investor USDC flows into the vault. The agent allocates across tokenized credits and continuously re-scores exposures as new signals arrive.

→
STAGE 4

Disclose

Every score change, reallocation, or risk alert is written onchain and mirrored to Reckon's X account — a public, timestamped decision log.
